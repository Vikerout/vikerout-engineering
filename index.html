ABOUT VIKEROUT

Independent. Flexible. Project-focused.

VIKEROUT Engineering is an independent project-based engineering practice providing piping and plant detail engineering support directly to clients, EPC contractors and engineering companies.

Core engineering activities are managed directly through VIKEROUT. For larger or specialized project scopes, additional experienced engineering professionals can be coordinated according to project requirements, technical scope and schedule.

This flexible model allows engineering capacity to be matched to the actual needs of each project.
